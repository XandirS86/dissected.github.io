about this site

This is where I publish analyses of whatever I am currently working through – football statistics, education, economics, politics. The subject changes, the method does not: one hypothesis, disclosed data, a traceable breakdown and, at the end, an honest statement of how confident I am.

This site is a hobby. There are no ads, no subscription, no tracking and no cookies. New analyses appear every one to two weeks, each in German and English; to make sure you miss nothing, subscribe to the RSS.

the name

a schley is both: one Schley among several – and A. Schley. dissected is what happens here to the subject, and occasionally to the author.

objections

If you want to dissect this analysis, do it here – with claim, objection and evidence. No comment section: objections arrive by e-mail, the strongest are published, with a reply. Rule: The strongest objection is always published, even when it lands. When it lands, the analysis changes – visibly under “status” and in the confidence level.
