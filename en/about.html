about this site

This is where I publish analyses of whatever happens to occupy me. That might be pure sports statistics, the education system or economics. Perhaps a critical look at social influencers, or at the behaviour of public figures of our time who move me. Sometimes in breadth, sometimes in depth. Anything goes, nothing has to, and occasionally things are simply thought backwards.

At heart, I want to examine how we live together and to assess it critically. Occasionally with ideas on how it could be “better”. The analyses follow the methodology stated in every piece: data, sources, confidence. Those ideas do not: they are qualitative, normative and accordingly less robust. An extrapolation of what exists, in the direction of mutual fairness. I label which is which.

What matters to me: this site is a hobby. No ads, no subscription, no tracking, no cookies. New analyses appear when they appear. If you want to miss something, do not subscribe to the RSS.

the name

a schley is both: one Schley among several – and A. Schley. dissected is what happens here to the subject, and occasionally to the author.

objections

If you want to dissect an analysis, do it here: with claim, objection and evidence. No comment section, objections arrive by e-mail. The rule: the strongest objection is published, with a reply, even when it lands. When it lands, the analysis changes, visibly under “status” and in the confidence level.
